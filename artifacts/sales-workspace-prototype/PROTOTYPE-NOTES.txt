LIMITATIONS
Document viewing is an accessible local HTML facsimile, not an integrated PDF viewer or download service.
Forms and all upload/save/submit/approve/query/payment/send actions are inert by design.
Role selection only previews the existing permission rules against fictional content; it does not change authentication.
Supporting modules are local placeholders; only the seven sale destinations are represented.
This prototype demonstrates UX and navigation, not production readiness or workflow integration.
