Focused panel refinement

Open the existing Comments & activity control in a full-page sale workspace. At desktop widths of 1200 pixels and above the panel occupies a right-hand grid column and the task reflows beside it. It is not a modal and does not cover task content. The supporting Completion checklist follows the task when space is limited. Closing the panel restores the previous task width.

On tablet and mobile the panel follows the task in normal page flow; opening it moves focus to its heading and scrolls it into view. Comments and Activity views, close control and Escape work locally. Closing restores focus to the identity-bar control. Unit, building, role, scenario and history navigation clear the panel context. Document preview continues to work independently. Sending and read-state updates remain unavailable.
