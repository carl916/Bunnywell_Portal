Stage 3 entry point
Call workspace_commands.enqueue_notification(event_id, stable_source_key, sale_id,
comment_id?, newly_added_mentions?) from the same transaction as each successful
authorised workflow transition. Expand its explicit three-event allowlist and the
renderer for the remaining 14 catalogue events; keep the stable identifiers and
existing saved overrides. Do not enqueue from mutable-state polling or synthetic
sale_mention_notifications rows. Keep the sale attempt UUID as the batching key.
The worker uses Stage 1's selection function on transaction-time audience facts and
resolveEmailNotificationRecipients again immediately before capture. The database
rechecks the current context/revision and exact remaining items at the capture boundary.
Persistent event/source identities, per-recipient items, fixed windows, expiring lease
and unique batch capture identities should remain the common path for future events.
Real delivery is outside this stage. If added later, preserve capture-only staging and
use provider idempotency/reconciliation principles from the existing sales-legal path.
