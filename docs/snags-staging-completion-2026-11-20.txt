Legacy image reads now have a bounded 20 MiB cap; the existing 4 MiB new-upload limit remains unchanged. Existing origin, redirect, timeout, input-pixel and image-output safeguards remain. The actual Tap drip image now produces an authenticated 128px WebP with HTTP 200.
